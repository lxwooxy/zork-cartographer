          Zork Cartographer

          Play Zork I and watch the world map itself as you go. Everything runs in your browser: the game, the map and the autopilot. Your game is saved in this browser after every move, so you can close the tab and come back.

          Click a room to plan a route, double-click to walk there. Save makes a checkpoint; Load goes back to one (the map keeps what you've explored). Reveal map shows every room (spoilers).
